Microphone & tone fallback

When voice is enabled, say “Hey Monty, controls.” You can also say pause, resume, next, or previous after the same wake phrase. Audio stays on the Pi and is not saved.

The experimental tone only opens the QR panel. It must be enabled on the Pi. Keep your phone volume modest.
